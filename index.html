Jais: Arabic-centric open models

2023–2026

Open foundation and chat models for Arabic and English, built with Inception and partners. My work covered evaluation, training data and training.

  • Arabic evaluation. Implemented and ran Arabic benchmarks, including ACVA, Al-Ghafa, Arabic MMLU and BoolQA Arabic, to compare Jais with other open models.
  • Safety and helpfulness evaluation. Built GPT-4-as-judge pipelines and curated safety and helpfulness evaluation sets in English and Arabic.
  • DPO training data. Curated and prepared preference data for DPO training.
  • Training. Ran DPO experiments on LLaMA-2 based Arabic models, and trained maths-specialized models (see Mathematical reasoning below).
  • Deployment. Served models with function calling using vLLM, Hugging Face and TGI, with Gradio interfaces.

Nanda: Hindi open-weights models

2024–2026

Open-weight bilingual Hindi and English models built on Llama 3 and Llama 3.1. The EACL 2026 paper reports the following.

  • A tokenizer extended with 20% Hindi-specific tokens, halving Hindi tokenization cost while keeping English efficiency.
  • Hindi-first continual pretraining with Llama Pro on a 65-billion-token corpus covering Devanagari, code-mixed and Romanized Hindi.
  • Bilingual instruction and safety alignment on a large, culturally grounded dataset.
  • Nanda-87B reports state-of-the-art results on summarization, translation, transliteration and instruction following. Both models report state-of-the-art safety and cultural knowledge.
  • Evaluation. Built and maintained the Hindi evaluation setup, handed the Hindi LM Harness code to university partners, and resolved evaluation dataset issues such as punctuation and ROUGE scoring.

Domain LLM for energy and Oil & Gas

Nov 2024 – present

Led the data curation, fine-tuning and evaluation pipeline for a domain-specific LLM.

  • SFT data curation pipeline. A multi-agent GPT-4 pipeline generates questions and answers from industry documents, with verifiers for specificity and correctness. Training data is selected by instruction-following difficulty, and deduplicated and merged with general domain data.
  • Evaluation data curation pipeline. A domain and subdomain taxonomy, checked with four LLMs, guides question generation. Answers are verified, checked for contamination against training data, and validated by subject-matter experts.
  • Distributed GRPO training. Multi-node reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards for function calling, text-to-SQL, structured output and instruction following. Resolved generation-server timeouts, distributed broadcast errors and NCCL errors.
  • Large-scale pre-training data preparation. Processed 343 billion words of technical text into 18 billion words of English, Oil & Gas relevant data, using language filtering, rule-based cleaning and fuzzy deduplication across 99 machines.
  • DPO training data preparation. Generated rejected responses for 59K question–answer pairs and built multi-pair preference data, producing 600K DPO pairs.
  • Model training and merging. SFT on LLaMA 4 Scout, SLERP model merging, and diagnosis of generation degeneration.

2026

LLM ensembles for vulnerability detection

Oct–Nov 2026

Applied research on AI agents and LLM ensembles that find vulnerabilities in production source code.

  • Agent harnesses. Evaluated six harnesses for code investigation, including Codex Security, VISA, Codex, OpenCode, OpenHands and Mentis.
  • Ensemble strategies. Aggregate Council (union of findings, for coverage), Majority Vote (conservative), and Neo Council (an LLM reviewer that keeps or drops each finding).
  • OWASP Java benchmark. Best recall of 80 of 88 vulnerable servlets, with Neo Council verification on one harness.
  • CWE-Bench-Java. Full-project scans on 120 real CVEs, with no hints about file or CVE.
  • SEC-bench Pro. A three-model council generated working proof-of-concept exploits for 86 of 207 JavaScript engine cases (41.5%).
  • CyberGym. 66% pass rate on 100 reproduction tasks with the best open-weight model.
