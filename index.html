About

I build large language models for Arabic, Hindi and specialized domains, and work on the engineering that makes them reliable in use.

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Inception Institute of AI, UAE, where I have worked since April 2023. My team develops large language models for Arabic and Hindi. We open-sourced Jais, an Arabic-centric model family, and Nanda, a Hindi model.

Most of my recent work is on the full path from data to a deployed model. That includes preference optimization and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (DPO, GRPO), automated evaluation platforms, domain-specific models for energy and Oil & Gas, and agents that use tools such as SQL engines, code interpreters and search. I also work on distributed training for models with more than 100 billion parameters.

Earlier applied projects include deep-learning weather and climate downscaling (2024) and the use of LLM ensembles to find vulnerabilities in production code (2026).

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Lille and INRIA, Lille (2017–2021), working with the Magnet team on discourse understanding, temporal relations and bridging anaphora. Before that I was a Project Scientist at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, and a Senior Member Technical Staff at Oracle India in Bangalore. I hold an M.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from IIT Kanpur and a B.Tech. in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from SGGS Nanded.

Applied research and engineering

In progress

Domain-specific LLM for energy and Oil & Gas

2024–2026 · Data, training and evaluation

Led the data curation, fine-tuning and evaluation pipeline for a domain LLM. Built multi-agent question-and-answer generation from industry documents, selected training data by instruction-following difficulty, and created evaluation sets validated by subject-matter experts.

Preference optimization and reinforcement learning

2023–2025 · DPO, GRPO and reward modeling

Preference data generation and DPO training, reward models, and multi-node GRPO training with verifiable rewards for function calling, text-to-SQL, structured output and instruction following.

Automated LLM evaluation platform

2023–2026 · Evaluation infrastructure

Began with extensions to the LM Evaluation Harness and GPT-4-as-judge pipelines, and grew into an automated platform that detects new model checkpoints and runs evaluations on Arabic, safety, math, code, function calling, SQL and domain tasks.

Agents that use tools

2024–2026 · Agentic AI and reinforcement learning

ReAct agents with calculators and code execution (2024). In 2026, training environments with sandboxed execution for SQL, Python, bash, knowledge-graph and wiki-search tools, and reward functions that check outcomes.

Distributed training of large models

2025 · Multi-node training

Analysis of ZeRO-3 communication overhead on multi-node clusters, and a hybrid parallelism setup with sharding inside each node and data parallelism across nodes, for models of more than 100 billion parameters.

Large-scale pre-training data pipeline

2025 · Data engineering

Built a pipeline that reduced 343 billion words of scientific and technical text to 18 billion words of high-quality English, domain-relevant data, using language filtering, rule-based cleaning and fuzzy deduplication at scale.

Weather and climate downscaling

2024 · Deep learning for earth science

Downscaling from 25 km reanalysis data (ERA5) to 2 km and 200 m WRF grids, with diffusion models (DDPM, DDIM and CorrDiff) and a FastAPI service that returns forecast videos and NetCDF files.

LLM ensembles for vulnerability detection

2026 · Applied security research

Applied research on AI agents and ensembles of LLMs to find vulnerabilities in production source code. Evaluated on public security benchmarks, including OWASP Java, CWE-Bench-Java and CyberGym.
